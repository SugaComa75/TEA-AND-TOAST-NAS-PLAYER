Tea & Toast NAS Player 0.1.4
================================

Mobile fixes
------------
- Added safe-area-aware spacing below the fixed player and extra content clearance.
- Added a visible close control to the mobile navigation drawer.
- Mobile browser Back now closes the open drawer before leaving the page.
- Tapping outside the drawer closes it.
- Background page scrolling is locked while the drawer is open and restored afterward.

Scope
-----
- Desktop navigation and player layout are unchanged.
- No colour enhancement was added.
- No artist or album image support was added.
