Tea & Toast NAS Player 0.1.4 - Mobile UAT
================================================

Preparation
-----------
1. Back up the current player files.
2. Replace the application files with the 0.1.4 package, preserving config/local.php and private data.
3. Sign in on a phone or a browser using a viewport no wider than 760px.

Test steps
----------
1. Scroll the library page and confirm the player does not cover the final content.
2. Confirm there is visible space between the player and the browser/phone bottom edge.
3. Tap the menu button. Confirm the drawer opens and the page behind it does not scroll.
4. Tap the X. Confirm the drawer closes and the previous page scroll position is restored.
5. Open the drawer and tap the shaded area outside it. Confirm the drawer closes.
6. Open the drawer and use the phone/browser Back action. Confirm only the drawer closes.
7. Use Back once more. Confirm normal browser navigation resumes.
8. Open the drawer and select a library, Random Play, or Admin. Confirm the drawer closes.
9. At a viewport wider than 760px, confirm the desktop sidebar and fixed player retain their previous layout.

Completion criteria
-------------------
- All five mobile fixes work without changing desktop behaviour.
- Music playback continues normally while opening and closing the drawer.
- No colour or image feature is present in this release.
